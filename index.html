Critical: JavaScript-Only Rendering

The site is built on Magento PWA Venia with no SSR configured. Search engines see a blank page. This single issue suppresses all organic traffic and mobile performance simultaneously — the highest-urgency fix in the entire audit.

🏆

The Core Opportunity

45 years of brand equity, dominant KSA market position, and an architecturally capable PWA platform. The gap between brand reputation and digital execution is the central challenge — and the central opportunity. Fixing it is a question of execution, not brand.

✅

Three Strategic Priorities

  • Performance & Technical: Fix JS rendering, implement SSR to unlock SEO and mobile speed
  • Conversion Optimization: Add trust signals, reviews, BNPL, abandoned cart recovery
  • Brand Elevation: Make the digital experience as premium as the physical stores

Section 09

Final Strategic Recommendation

AJStore.com has the most powerful asset in Saudi e-commerce: a trusted, heritage brand with genuine product quality. The digital channel is not performing at the level this brand deserves.

⚡

Fix the Foundation First

Technical issues are the ceiling on every other improvement. No CRO strategy overcomes a 5-second load time. SSR in the first 30 days simultaneously unlocks SEO and mobile conversions — the single highest-ROI action in this audit.

📈

Convert Before Spending More

Reviews, BNPL, abandoned cart sequences, and guest checkout increase conversion rates by 50–150% in apparel without increasing ad spend. Maximize the value of existing traffic before scaling acquisition budgets.

🏆

Build a Brand-Worthy Digital Home

Ajlan & Brothers is a premium institution. The long-term vision should be an editorial, storytelling-rich experience that celebrates Saudi sartorial heritage — not just another online shop. Match the physical store experience digitally.
